The Golden Sanctuary

Before the first kingdoms there was Aurelia: temples on floating islands, golden bridges between sanctuaries and rivers of light.

At its center beat the Heart of Eternity, which kept six primordial forces in balance: Sun, Wisdom, Depth, Flame, Life and Spirit.

The Fall

Then something came from beyond the edge of reality. It was neither god nor mortal. It ate memories, twisted magic and turned light into shadow. The guardians called it the Hollow.

As it spread, the Heart cracked. The guardians broke the Heart into six sacred relics, hid them across the islands, and raised the Veil, a barrier that sealed Aurelia away from the world.

The Hollow was imprisoned, and Aurelia was forgotten.

The Awakening

Centuries later the Veil is thinning. Lyra Venn, a young explorer mapping ruins in the mortal world, finds a glowing teal shard in a collapsed temple.

When she touches it, the shard opens a door in the sky. On the other side is Aurelia, half in ruins.

The shard is a fragment of the Heart. It chose her.

The Call

At the first temple a small guardian spirit made of crystal light wakes up and joins her: Glim, the last of the sanctuary's keepers.

The surviving guardians tell Lyra the six relics must be recovered before the Hollow breaks free.

Characters

Chunky stylized proportions and simple faceted faces. Lyra is attractive and athletic, tasteful, never sexualized.

Lyra Venn Hero, 22, explorer. Confident and friendly: teal-dyed bob, sand explorer jacket, gold compass, the Heart shard glowing on a cord.
Glim Companion: a small floating fox-like spirit of teal crystal facets, a gold rune on its forehead, a trail of light for a tail.
Elder Oro Guardian and quest giver: an ancient sandstone statue come to life, with gold Celtic knots, glowing teal eyes and a stone beard.

The Hollow & Its Creatures

On every island the Hollow's creatures guard the relic. Lyra fights them with the gems' power, and every victory rebuilds part of the sanctuary.

But the deeper she goes, the clearer one truth becomes: the Hollow was not only imprisoned. It was guarding something, and Aurelia's greatest secret is still waiting behind the Veil.

Hollow Wisp Common enemy: floating dark violet smoke with magenta eyes.
Shade Golem Strong enemy: cracked sandstone golem with violet crystals.
Veil Wraith Elite enemy: tall hooded shadow, torn cloak, crystal claws.
The Hollow Final boss: a huge mask-like face of black and violet crystal facets with one great magenta eye.

Core Loop & Interface

A young explorer travels across Aurelia's floating islands. Every match on the gem board powers her attacks on the Hollow's creatures and restores the sanctuaries.

1. Match Gems of the six forces: Sun, Wisdom, Depth, Flame, Life, Spirit.
2. Charge Each gem color fills one of Lyra's powers. Full powers fire at the creature above the board.
3. Defeat Beat the creature within a set number of moves to win the level.
4. Restore Ruins rebuild and waterfalls flow again on the world map.
5. Recover One of the six relics at the end of each island.

Style Guide: Low-Poly 3D

Low polygon count, large flat facets and hard edges, like a polished toy diorama. Warm sun from the top left, cool blue fill from the right, bloom only on teal magic and gems.

  • Low polygon count, large flat facets, hard edges. No smooth surfaces, no fine texture.
  • Flat shading with soft ambient occlusion. Bloom only on teal magic and gems.
  • No outlines. Shape comes from facets and light.
  • Gems and items are faceted 3D objects, never painted icons.
  • UI uses the same 3D material: beveled gold or teal buttons, faceted sandstone panels with gold frames.
  • Avoid painterly brushwork, thick outlines, photorealism, high-poly surfaces, tiny details and neon.
