Design Pillars

Genre Casual side-scrolling run-and-gun shooter. Landscape gameplay, portrait menus and art.
Tone Grim but readable. Monumental and oppressive, with a hot ember of hope. Never gory.
Art direction Stylized, chunky gothic dieselpunk: brutalist monuments, cathedral-factories, giant walkers
Hook Burn the Dominion banner at the end of a level and the district rises up to fight beside you.
The Ascendant Dominion

For a thousand years the Ascendant Dominion has ruled the world of Vharos from the Spire of the Undying, a cathedral-fortress taller than the clouds. At its top sits the God-Chancellor, a ruler kept alive inside a throne of pipes and engines, whose stone face watches from every square.

The Dominion turned cities into cathedral-factories: gothic arches over endless assembly lines, choirs singing over loudspeakers, and walker tanks the size of chapels marching between monuments. Crimson banners with its emblem, the Crowned Iron Eye, hang from every wall.

The Hero

Roan Kross, 32, is a former Dominion officer who defected after he was ordered to burn his own home district. Broad-shouldered and rugged, with dark stubble and a scar across one cheek.

His left arm is a heavy dieselpunk prosthetic of riveted iron and pistons, rebuilt from a Dominion walker. He wears scratched heavy armor with the Dominion emblem scraped off and an orange ember painted over it, a long patched coat and an orange scarf, and carries a rotary rifle.

His companion is Wick, a salvaged hovering drone with one warm orange lens, once a Dominion surveillance unit until Roan rewired it.

Style Guide

Stylized 2.5D mobile game art with chunky heroic proportions and slightly oversized armor. Cold grey light from above, red lenses and stained glass, warm orange fire, searchlights, smoke and embers.

Oil Black#0F0F11 · Shadows, silhouettes
Gunmetal#2B2D33 · Background, armor
Concrete#6E6B66 · Monuments, fog, smoke
Dominion Crimson#A3191F · Banners, the Iron Eye
Tarnished Gold#B8913A · Ornaments, Iron Saints
Bone Ivory#E3D8BF · Statues, highlights
Ember Orange#FF7A2A · Rebel paint, fire, UI
Spark Yellow#FFC65C · Fire cores, sparks
  • Crimson and gold always mean the Dominion. Orange fire always means the rebels. Never mix them on one character.
  • The Dominion is vertical, symmetrical, spiky and gothic. The rebels are rounded, patched and asymmetric.
  • Thick oil-black outlines on characters, machines and items.
  • The empire is always bigger than the frame.
  • The only empire symbol is the Crowned Iron Eye: a mechanical eye under a spiked crown inside a gear ring.
  • Avoid gore, real-world political symbols or insignia, real uniforms, franchise looks, neon and tiny details.
