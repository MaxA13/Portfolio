The World

Nine realms hang from the branches of Yggdrasil, the great tree whose roots drink from the wells of fate. For ages its glow held the cold at bay. Now the glow is failing. Fimbulwinter, the winter before the end of the world, has begun to creep along the roots, and frost spreads from realm to realm. Runes that once blazed on every stone and blade are going dark.

The Theft

The frost has a king. Thrym, lord of the Frost Giants of Jotunheim, has stolen Mjolnir, the hammer of Thor, and hidden it deep in his ice fortress. Without the hammer, the gods of Asgard cannot hold the winter back, and the thunder that once kept the giants in check has gone silent. Thrym wants more than the hammer. He wants the tree itself, and a world of endless ice.

Far below, in the roots, something else stirs. Fenrir, the great wolf, has shaken loose the last strands of Gleipnir, the ribbon that bound him. Odin's ravens, Huginn and Muninn, fly the nine realms and carry the news back: the old bindings are breaking.

The Hero

Haldor is no god and no prince. He is a stocky axe-bearer from Midgard, a village at the foot of the tree, with a braided red beard and a stubborn streak. When the frost reaches his home, the gods have no answer, so he picks up his axe and walks the rune road toward the tree.

Haldor cannot light the runes alone, but he can forge. Salvaged blades, shards of old rune-steel and relics of the gods can be joined, two into one, into stronger weapons. Each merge wakes a rune. Each rune brings back a little of the fire.

Haldor
The Relics

Along the road lie the treasures of the gods, scattered by the winter: Gjallarhorn, the horn that will announce Ragnarok. Draupnir, the ring that drips new rings. The Helm of Awe. Freya's necklace Brisingamen. Gleipnir's last threads. The golden apples of Idunn. Each one carries power Haldor can borrow, and each one has a price.

The Journey

Midgard is the home base. Beyond it lie the realms, one chapter at a time: Jotunheim and its king, the dwarven forges of Svartalfheim, the mist of Niflheim, the fires of Muspelheim, and, further on, Helheim and the golden halls of Asgard. Every realm has a guardian. Every guardian guards a piece of the tree's fading light.

Win the realms back, and the tree wakes. Fail, and the winter finishes what it began.
