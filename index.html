03 · The Codex

Designing with a Codex.

CodexLab is named after the rules I work by. Honest, transparent, human-first UX. No dark patterns.

  1. I

    No dark patterns

    No tricks, no hidden costs, no guilt-trips. If a flow only works by confusing people, it doesn't ship.

  2. II

    Transparent by default

    People should always know what is happening, why, and what it will cost them.

  3. III

    Human-first

    Interfaces that respect people's time, attention and money, and are genuinely good for them.

  4. IV

    Results that last

    Trust is the metric behind the metrics. Honest design still has to move the numbers, and it does.

04 · Human expertise + AI

Experience augmented

With 15+ years in UX, I combine human intuition and critical decision-making with the speed of AI. I don't let tools design for me; I use them to work faster and explore further.

01

Strategic direction

Every project starts from your business goals. AI generates options; my judgment picks the right direction.

02

Brand & audience fit

Generative tools produce endless possibilities. I listen to the brand and its audience, then shape those ideas into something coherent and emotionally resonant: design that doesn’t just work, but lasts.

03

Ethical design

Design that’s honest: no dark patterns, transparent, and genuinely good for the user. I use generative tools responsibly, never to manipulate.

04

Critical decision-making

Algorithms can predict, but people empathize. Every wireframe, micro-interaction and user flow is checked against years of watching how real people use technology.

05

Human-crafted refinement

AI explores hundreds of directions fast. I bring the taste and brand intuition to pick the right one, then polish every detail, from typography rhythm to animation easing, until it feels unmistakably yours.
