01 · The Concept

Invent your defense

Defend a floating clockwork city from a sky armada by building and upgrading brass towers along its sky-bridges.

ToneCozy workshops against a sky full of smoke and iron
HookMerge salvaged parts to unlock new tower designs
02 · The City

The last clean city in the sky

Copper towers, gear-driven bridges and clock faces, held aloft by the Amber Heart, a giant living crystal that powers every engine in Cogspire.

Below the clouds, the Soot Armada rises to rip the Heart out and burn it in their furnaces.

03 · The Heroes

Garrick & Wren

Garrick Vane + TickThe heavy hitter: jetpack, rivet cannon and a clockwork hawk that spots ships
Wren Halloway + CogThe quick builder: glider pack, big wrench and a clockwork owl
04 · The Towers

Four gadgets, three tiers

Steam CannonHeavy single target · Squat copper barrel puffing steam
Tesla SpireChain lightning · Tall coil with amber arcs
Gear ThrowerFast, hits several · Spinning wheel launching saw-gears
Amber BeaconSlows enemies, buffs · Lighthouse lamp glowing amber
05 · Core Loop

Defend. Build. Invent.

DefendStop ships on fixed sky lanes
BuildTowers on brass platforms, 3 tiers each
WorkshopMerge parts into new blueprints
Boss siegesA dreadnought every few levels
06 · The Soot Armada

Smoke, iron and furnace glow

Iron airships with smokestacks, gas-masked sky-pirates and clockwork drones with red-amber eyes.

07 · The Map

Outer rim to core

  1. 1 · Sunrise Docks – moorings and cranes
  2. 2 · Clocktower Quarter – under giant clock faces
  3. 3 · The Great Foundry – molten copper
  4. 4 · Stormgear Heights – lightning rods
  5. 5 · The Heart Chamber – a cathedral of brass
08 · The Final Boss

The Iron Maw

Flagship of the Soot Armada: a dreadnought the size of a city district, with a furnace mouth for a prow and grates glowing like teeth.

09 · Style Guide

Copper, brass and amber

Round and friendly for the city, heavy and spiky for the Armada. Warm amber means powered and friendly; red-orange furnace glow means enemy. Thick soot-black outlines on characters and towers.
