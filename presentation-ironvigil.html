01 · The Concept

One rebel against an empire

A lone rebel soldier runs and guns his way through a colossal gothic war-empire, from its ash mines to the throne at the top of its spire.

ToneGrim but readable, with a hot ember of hope
Art directionStylized, chunky gothic dieselpunk
HookBurn the banner, and the district rises with you
02 · The Ascendant Dominion

A thousand years of iron

The Dominion rules Vharos from the Spire of the Undying. Its cities are cathedral-factories, its walker tanks are the size of chapels, and the Crowned Iron Eye hangs from every wall.

At the top sits the God-Chancellor, kept alive inside a throne of pipes and engines.

03 · The Hero

Roan Kross & Wick

A former Dominion officer who defected when ordered to burn his own home district. Iron prosthetic arm, the emblem scraped off his armor and an orange ember painted over it.

Wick is a rewired Dominion surveillance drone with one warm orange lens.

07 · The Final Boss

The Basilica Rex

A colossal spider-like walker with a gothic cathedral on its back, a red rose-window eye and organ-pipe cannons. The God-Chancellor leads the final battle from inside it.

08 · Style Guide

Crimson empire, orange fire

Crimson and gold always mean the Dominion; orange fire always means the rebels. The Dominion is vertical and spiky, the rebels rounded and patched. Thick oil-black outlines throughout.
